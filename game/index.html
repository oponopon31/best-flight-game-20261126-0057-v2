AERIS

RIDGE RUN

HOW TO PLAY — Fly the valley. Thread the glowing rings, mind your fuel, and bring her home in one piece. Too slow and she stalls; too rough and the ridge wins.

Shift / W throttle up
Ctrl / S throttle down
↑ / ↓ pitch
← / → or A / D bank & turn
Q / E rudder
F flaps   B brake
C camera   M mute
P pause   R restart

Mouse drag looks around and steers a little. Sound starts on your first click or key.
